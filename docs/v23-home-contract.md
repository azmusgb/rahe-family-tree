# Family Home 3.0 contract

Home is a launch surface, not a dashboard dump. Mobile order: family identity/hero -> Explore the family tree -> Continue/recently viewed -> Discover. Secondary preview stacks stay collapsed on phones and restore on desktop.

Do not duplicate the same destination repeatedly merely to fill the page. Search remains below hero/context. Canonical story, person, media and research content remains available through its owning route even when its Home preview is collapsed.
