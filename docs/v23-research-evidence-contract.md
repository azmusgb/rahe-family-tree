# Evidence-native research UX contract

Research surfaces must preserve canonical claim state exactly: SUPPORTED, PROVISIONAL, UNRESOLVED, and REJECTED. Presentation work may expose or explain those states but must never promote or reconcile them.

Person facts should expose claim -> state -> sources -> conflicts/reasoning. Tree nodes may expose compact evidence indicators, but the graph remains a view over canonical relationships rather than a second source of truth.

The Research Command Center should prioritize unresolved identities, provisional relationships, conflicting claims, missing vital records, source gaps, quarantined/rejected assertions, and queued research targets. Every item should deep-link to the relevant Person, Source, Evidence, or Tree context.
